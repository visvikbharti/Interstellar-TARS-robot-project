Useful swings on that number:
- **Pi 5 4GB instead of 8GB:** −₹7,370 (→ ~₹22,100). But this build's pipeline *is* on-device speech models (sherpa-onnx STT + Piper TTS), so by that criterion 8GB is the right buy — and the only in-stock 4GB source is The Engineer Store (see the caution at the end).
- **DS3218 20kg instead of MG996R** (6×): +₹10,848 (→ ~₹40,300). Only worth it if the legs actually need 20 kg·cm; for a 25–30cm TARS, MG996R is usually sufficient and DS3218 is out of stock at the cheap vendor anyway.
- **LX-16A serial bus servos** (6×): +₹10,848, and you'd drop the PCA9685 (−₹232). Big win in wiring simplicity and position feedback, big hit to budget.
- **Skip the SPI screen, use USB mic instead of I2S:** −₹371 (screen −427, INMP441 −146, USB mic +202).

## (b) Hard to get in India — and what to use instead

1. **A2-class 64GB microSD.** Every hobby vendor stocks the SanDisk **Ultra**, which is **A1**, not A2. → Buy a SanDisk **Extreme** A2 on Amazon.in (~₹1,800–2,200), or just accept A1 — for a robot that boots and runs Python it makes almost no practical difference.
2. **Cheap MAX98357A breakout.** All four generic variants are **out of stock at robu.in**; only DFRobot (₹531–669) and Adafruit (₹730) branded boards are available, 3–6× the usual price. → Robokits lists a generic at ₹89 (worth a stock check), or drop I2S entirely and use a **PAM8403 amp (₹29, Robocraze)** with a USB audio dongle.
3. **DS3218 20kg servos.** Out of stock at robu.in (both variants, the cheapest source). → **ThinkRobotics ₹2,099.99** is the only sane in-stock option; Probots at ₹4,150 is not worth it.
4. **LX-16A serial bus servos.** Single source (ThinkRobotics, ₹2,099.99). → **Waveshare SC15 17kg at ₹1,957.82** (Engineer Store) or **ST3215 at ₹2,399.99** are equivalent-class bus servos with better availability.
5. **Genuine Samsung/LG 18650 cells.** Samsung 30Q (₹578) and LG MJ1 (₹392) are both **out of stock** at robu.in. → **Murata/Sony VTC6 ₹691** or **Panasonic NCR18650GA ₹635**, both in stock. Better still, buy the **pre-built 2S packs** (Molicel ₹999 / Samsung 35E ₹899 / BAK ₹799) — genuine cells, already tabbed and wired, and it removes the spot-welding problem entirely.
6. **8mm aluminium rod/tube.** Hobby vendors barely stock raw stock; only the ThinkRobotics 300mm variant is available. → For a Delhi buyer this is the one item to buy **offline**: Lajpat Rai Market / Bhagirath Palace (Chandni Chowk) hardware stalls sell aluminium rod by the foot for a fraction of the online price.
7. **M3 screw/nut assortment kit.** Nobody sells a good cheap assortment — robu sells per-size 12-piece sets (₹71–175 each), and the Engineer Store's 200-piece kit is ₹1,050.60. → An Amazon.in M3 assortment box (~₹400–600) is better value, or buy loose from Lajpat Rai Market.

**Two design gotchas worth flagging before you order:**

- **TP4056 cannot charge your 2S pack.** It is a 1S (4.2V) charger. For a 7.4V pack you need an **8.4V CC/CV adapter feeding the 2S BMS**, or a dedicated 2S charger. Buying a TP4056 for this build is a dead end.
- **Don't run servos off the 5V/5A buck.** Six MG996Rs can stall at ~2.5A each. A 5A module is sized for the Pi alone. Give the servos their **own** high-current rail — a 6V UBEC of 8A+ — with a fat electrolytic across the servo rail. (Post-audit correction: the ₹189 module first suggested for the Pi rail turned out to be 9–24V-input and can't run from 2S at all; the corrected two-UBEC chain is in `hardware/BOM.md`.)

## (c) Vendor consolidation for a Delhi buyer

**Anchor on robu.in (Pune).** It carries ~₹24,900 of this BOM in stock — the Pi, PSU, cooler, battery pack, BMS, both buck converters, the INMP441, the SPI display, bearings, M3 hardware and filament. Free shipping over ₹999 (well clear), ships within 24h via FedEx/Aramex, 2–5 working days to Delhi. It also has the most trustworthy stock data of any vendor here — it shows real per-SKU In Stock / Out of Stock, which is why it caught the PCA9685 and DS3218 shortages.

**Add Robocraze (Bangalore) as the second order** — ₹3,520 for the three things robu can't do: the **PCA9685** (out of stock at robu), the **correct 180° MG996R** (robu's is continuous-rotation), and the microSD (₹323 cheaper). Free delivery over ₹999, COD over ₹500.

Those two cover about 96% of the build. To avoid a third and fourth parcel:

- Buy the **DFRobot MAX98357 from robu at ₹669** instead of Engineer Store at ₹531 — costs ₹138 more, saves a separate shipment.
- Take robu's **8Ω speaker at ₹120** instead of the 4Ω from Engineer Store, or source a speaker locally.
- Get the **8mm aluminium tube offline in Delhi** rather than a ₹250 item shipped from ThinkRobotics.

That collapses the build to a clean **two-vendor buy: robu.in + Robocraze**, roughly ₹25,700 + ₹3,520.

**A caution on The Engineer Store.** Its prices are frequently the lowest in the table (Pi 5 8GB at ₹17,967, DFRobot amp at ₹531), but the Pi 5 listing shows an inflated "₹37,100 → 52% off" MRP and the page itself warns *"Quantity selected exceeds current stock."* I also could not find any "tax included" labelling on their product pages, and their odd price decimals (₹441.91, ₹1,649.89) are consistent with a pre-GST or formula-derived figure. Confirm the landed, GST-inclusive price and real stock before committing a ₹18,000 order there. For the ₹800 of small parts, the risk is negligible.
